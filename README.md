**DUNESAIL** — arena survival on a living desert. Pilot a sand-skiff across three realms (Dune Sea, Glass Flats, Sunken Court), slay seven kinds of desert foe, level up through 14 upgrades, claim 9 relics with pair synergies, spend sun-shards in the workshop between realms, and defeat two bosses — the Dune Tyrant sandworm and the Glass Regent — before sailing on into Endless mode. Three difficulties, procedural WebAudio sound and music, combos, damage numbers, particles, screen shake, hazards per realm, and a full loop: title, HUD, pause, level-up choices, workshop, victory and game-over screens with restart and a persistent best score.

WASD/arrows to sail, mouse to aim, hold click/Space to fire the sunlance, Shift to dash, B for sun-bombs, Esc to pause, M to mute. Everything — art, sound, music, names — is original and made in code; the game runs fully offline from static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
